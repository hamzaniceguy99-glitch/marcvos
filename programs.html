What we do, in detail

Instruction, working with minors, supervision and contact, and our limits.

Program 01 · Matched to the person

Sports instruction

Instruction built around who is actually in front of you rather than a programme applied to everyone. Progression is paced to the age and the level, which for younger participants means less load and more technique than most people expect. No result, selection or improvement is guaranteed, because those depend on far more than coaching, and anyone promising them is describing a hope.

  • Per participant
  • Age appropriate
  • Level appropriate
  • No results promised

Program 02 · Ask us for it

Working with minors

Where an activity brings an adult into contact with minors, there is a specific criminal record extract intended for exactly that, and a parent is entitled to ask to see confirmation. Just as important is a named route by which a child or parent can raise a concern without it passing through the person it concerns, because a complaint that has to travel through its own subject never arrives. Concerns about a participant's integrity are acted on rather than handled quietly within the group.

  • Extract obtainable
  • Named route
  • Never informal
  • Parents told how

Program 03 · Explained first

Supervision and contact

Supervision is matched to age, level and the genuine risk of the activity, and we decline a group size or a setting where adequate supervision is not possible, which occasionally means turning work down. Physical contact needed for technique or safety is explained before it happens rather than simply done. Changing facilities, travel and residential activities are arranged so an adult is not alone with an individual child where that can be avoided.

  • Matched to risk
  • Contact explained
  • Not alone where avoidable
  • Told in advance

Program 04 · Health goes elsewhere

What we are not

Instruction is not a healthcare act. We do not diagnose, do not treat injury and do not give dietary, nutritional or medical advice, and anything about an injury or fitness to participate belongs with a doctor. Coaching qualifications are stated rather than implied, and they are not protected professional titles in Belgium, so nobody should read one as a guarantee. Check your own accident cover: ours and yours are different things.

  • No medical advice
  • Qualifications stated
  • No protected title claimed
  • Check your own cover
